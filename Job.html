Open positions

Front-End Developer, Accessible Learning Tools

Build the pages that students and teachers use every day, from lesson viewers to quiz screens, with accessibility checked at every step.

Reference number
LL101
Salary
AUD $78,000 to $92,000 per year plus superannuation
Reports to
Head of Product Engineering

Key responsibilities

  • Build and maintain accessible lesson, quiz and dashboard pages in HTML5 and CSS3.
  • Turn design files into layouts that work on phones, tablets and desktops.
  • Test every page with a keyboard and a screen reader before release.
  • Review teammates' code and fix issues found in accessibility audits.
  • Document reusable components so other teams can use them.

Essential requirements

  • HTML5 and semantic markup: write correct landmarks, headings, lists and forms.
  • CSS3 layout (Flexbox and Grid): build page layouts without frameworks.
  • Accessibility (WCAG 2.2): know how to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA.
  • Responsive design: design layouts that adapt to any screen size.

Preferable requirements

  • Git and GitHub: use branches and pull requests in a team.
  • Clear written communication: explain technical choices to non-technical colleagues.

UX and Accessibility Designer

Design clear, welcoming online learning experiences and make sure every design works for people with different abilities and devices.

Reference number
LL102
Salary
AUD $74,000 to $88,000 per year plus superannuation
Reports to
Head of Design

Key responsibilities

  • Design wireframes and prototypes for courses, assessments and progress screens.
  • Run usability tests with learners, including people who use assistive technology.
  • Write plain-language interface text and instructions.
  • Check colour contrast, focus order and reading order in every design.
  • Work with developers so the finished product matches the design.

Essential requirements

  • Accessibility (WCAG 2.2): apply WCAG 2.2 level AA to designs.
  • Wireframing and prototyping: turn ideas into wireframes and clickable prototypes.
  • User research and usability testing: plan sessions, observe learners and report findings.
  • Clear written communication: write short, clear instructions.

Preferable requirements

  • HTML5 and semantic markup: understand how a design becomes a web page.
  • CSS3 layout (Flexbox and Grid): know what is easy and hard to build.

Personal English Language Tutor

Support learners in building confidence, fluency and academic English skills through personalised, encouraging one-to-one sessions.

Reference number
LL103
Salary
AUD $82,000 to $98,000 per year plus superannuation
Reports to
Head of Academic Support

Key responsibilities

  • Plan and deliver personalised English lessons for students with different levels, goals and learning styles.
  • Teach speaking, listening, reading and writing in a clear and encouraging way.
  • Track learner progress, set achievable goals and provide constructive feedback.
  • Create engaging lesson materials, activities and homework to reinforce learning.
  • Support students in preparing for interviews, assessments, everyday conversation and academic communication.

Essential requirements

  • IELTS overall score of 6.5 or equivalent English proficiency qualification.
  • Strong spoken and written English communication skills.
  • Ability to design tailored lesson plans for individual learners.
  • Patience, empathy and the ability to motivate students of different ages and backgrounds.
  • Experience supporting learners with grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation or conversation skills.

Preferable requirements

  • TESOL, TEFL, ESL or equivalent teaching qualification.
  • Experience teaching online or in a one-to-one tutoring environment.

Mathematics Teacher (Primary to Lower Secondary)

Guide students from primary through lower secondary in building strong mathematical foundations, problem-solving confidence and consistent academic progress.

Reference number
LL104
Salary
AUD $86,000 to $102,000 per year plus superannuation
Reports to
Head of Curriculum and Learning

Key responsibilities

  • Teach mathematics to students across primary and lower secondary levels using clear explanations and structured lesson plans.
  • Build confidence in arithmetic, algebra, geometry, problem-solving and mathematical reasoning.
  • Prepare lessons, assessments and revision activities tailored to different learning levels.
  • Monitor learner progress, identify gaps in understanding and provide actionable support.
  • Work closely with families and academic staff to maintain consistent student development.

Essential requirements

  • Strong knowledge of mathematics curriculum from primary to lower secondary levels.
  • Ability to explain concepts clearly and adapt teaching methods for different learning needs.
  • Experience teaching or tutoring students in foundational and intermediate mathematics.
  • Excellent classroom communication, organisation and student engagement skills.

Preferable requirements

  • Teaching qualification or relevant education training.
  • Experience teaching in online, hybrid or small-group learning environments.
